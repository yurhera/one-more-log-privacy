Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game One More Log (“the Game”).

The developer respects your privacy and is committed to protecting user information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The Game itself does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, phone number, home address, or contacts.

However, the Game uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may automatically collect certain information.

This information may include:

IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;

device and advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID where applicable;

information about interactions with the Game and advertisements, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views;

diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, crashes, hangs, and other technical information;

advertising-related information required to display, measure, and protect advertisements.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertisements may include:

rewarded advertisements that users can voluntarily watch in exchange for an in-game reward;

interstitial advertisements displayed at certain points during gameplay.

Google and its advertising partners may process information about the device and interactions with advertisements for purposes such as:

displaying and delivering advertisements;

measuring advertising performance;

analytics;

preventing fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity;

complying with applicable legal requirements.

Depending on the user's region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

3. Rewarded Advertisements

The Game may allow users to voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to receive in-game benefits, such as additional bonuses, doubled rewards, or gameplay-related benefits.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional unless explicitly stated otherwise within the Game.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses Google advertising services.

Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles information from applications that use its services, please refer to Google's privacy documentation.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party advertising services may be used to:

provide advertisements;

provide rewarded advertising functionality;

measure advertisement performance;

analyze interactions with advertisements and the Game;

diagnose technical problems;

improve service reliability;

prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid activity.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Certain information may be transmitted to and processed by Google and its advertising technology partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention services.

7. Data Security

Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads services is protected in transit using industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections where applicable.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The Game does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database containing users' personal information.

Information collected by third-party services may be retained according to the policies and retention periods of those service providers.

Users may be able to reset or delete their Android Advertising ID through their device privacy settings.

For privacy or data-related questions concerning the Game, users may contact the developer using the contact information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising and data-processing practices may be adjusted according to the age classification of the Game, applicable laws, Google Play requirements, and Google advertising policies.

10. User Choices and Consent

Depending on the user's country or region, the Game may request consent or provide privacy choices related to advertising.

Users may also have privacy and advertising controls available through their device or Google account settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game, advertising services, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
